## 3. Visual & UX Upgrades (What Changed Visually)

1. **Modern High-Tech Aesthetic:**
   - Replaced the dated, washed-out layout with a deep space-navy foundation (`#070B14`, `#0E1626`) paired with energetic Sheffield Molten Orange (`#FF5500`), Electric Royal Blue (`#0062FF`), and Eco Green (`#10B981`).
   - Integrated custom fluid abstract gradient meshes and glowing tech lines to emphasize British high-tech engineering.

2. **Hero Conversion Powerhouse:**
   - Introduced an eye-catching 3D isometric representation of the NexGen Magnetic platform deck.
   - Added animated performance metric pills: `60s Assembly`, `7.8kg Ultralight`, `1500kg Load Tested`, `15 Yrs Lifespan`.
   - Clear dual Call-To-Action (CTA): **"Get Instant Stage Quote"** (primary) and **"Interactive Stage Designer"** (secondary).

3. **Side-by-Side Range Differentiation (Platinum vs SteelCity):**
   - Transformed the plain text list into two sleek, modern comparison cards showcasing price points (£269 + VAT vs £219 + VAT), specifications, and direct PDF assembly guide buttons.

4. **Interactive Magnetic Revolution Comparison:**
   - Created a side-by-side visual matrix highlighting the maintenance flaws of competitor mechanical locks (8 locks per deck, seized mechanisms) versus NexGen’s 100% zero-lock magnetic snap system.

5. **Organized Categories & Turnkey Packages:**
   - Restructured the 6 product categories into interactive glassmorphism cards with custom vector icons and hover transitions.
   - Added curated turnkey stage packages for quick ordering (3x2m, 3-Tier Choir, 5x3m).

6. **Full Responsive Optimization:**
   - Tailored breakpoints for Desktop (>1080px), Tablet (768px - 1080px), and Mobile (<768px).
   - Touch-friendly mobile drawer menu and floating sticky quick-quote pill.
